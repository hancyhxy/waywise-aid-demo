# Waywise: mid-fidelity prototype

The fidelity ladder:
1. **Low-fi** (`../low-fi/`, submitted 30 Sep): black-and-white wireframes of the v1 structure.
2. **Mid-fi** (this): iterated after the low-fi review and the team UX stories (Haruna, Beomhui). It adds Rushing / Normal / Tired states that expand into needs, standing vs seated time, four journey stages, a "Platform busier than shown?" report, and a "Worth it?" rating with approved learning. Visuals: greyscale with one blue accent; red and green carry status meaning only.
3. **Hi-fi** (`../v2/`): the same features in full visual design, used for the Prototype-in-Action video.

`index.html`, `app.js` and `styles.css` are copies of v2. `midfi.css` holds the mid-fi skin. All data is fictional.
Live: https://hancyhxy.github.io/waywise-aid-demo/mid-fi/
